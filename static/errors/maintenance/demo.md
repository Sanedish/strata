## What happened?

This is an issue on our side. Your browser and connection are fine.

A security flaw was published in software this service depends on. It lets attackers take over a server without
logging in. No fixed release exists yet, so we took the service offline.

## What does this mean?

- The website is offline.
- The streams are offline.
- The admin panel is offline.

Accounts and settings stay untouched.

## Who is affected?

All visitors and everyone who uses the admin panel.

## When will it return?

We expect to be back within a few days, once a fixed release is out. We will update this page if the plan changes.
